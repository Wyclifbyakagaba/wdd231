About Us

Our Mission

Fumu Sports Foundation empowers youth and communities through sport by providing training, mentorship, and opportunity across Uganda and East Africa.

Our Vision

To create pathways for talent, education, and community development through accessible sports programs and partnerships.

What We Do

  • Youth and adult sports training programs
  • Athlete rankings and performance tracking
  • Coach development and certification
  • Community outreach and sports events
  • Sponsorship and partnerships

Team

Our team includes certified coaches, program managers, and volunteers working across multiple regions to deliver sports development programs.
