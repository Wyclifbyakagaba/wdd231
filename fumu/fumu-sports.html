Fumu Sports Foundation – Site Plan

Site Name

Fumu Sports Foundation

The name "Fumu Sports Foundation" was chosen because it represents a sports development organization dedicated to athlete growth, community sports programs, youth empowerment, and charity initiatives.

Site Purpose

The website will provide structured information about sports training programs, athlete rankings, coaching staff, competitions, sponsorship opportunities, and charity sports initiatives including programs for street children and underprivileged youth.

Scenarios

  • Which sports programs can young athletes join in Uganda?
  • How are players ranked locally and internationally?
  • How can sponsors support sports development or charity programs?
  • Where can street children access free sports training?

Sports Programs

  • Football (Local & International leagues)
  • Basketball (Uganda & East Africa competitions)
  • Boxing & Kickboxing
  • Tennis & Table Tennis
  • Netball
  • Horse Racing
  • Ekigwo Traditional Wrestling
  • Gymnastics
  • Sports Dance & Fitness
  • Corporate Soccer Leagues

Competitions

  • Local Uganda leagues and tournaments
  • East Africa regional championships
  • International youth sports competitions
  • Street children global sports tournaments

Player Rankings

The website will display rankings of athletes by sport, region, and performance level, including comparisons across Uganda, East Africa, and international categories.

Coaching Staff

Profiles of certified coaches in each sport including boxing trainers, football coaches, athletics trainers, and youth development mentors.

Sponsors & Partners

  • Local sports academies
  • Corporate sponsors
  • International sports charities
  • Government sports bodies

Street Children Sports Program

A charity initiative offering free training, competitions, mentorship, and global exposure for street children through sports.

Athlete Lifestyle

The platform highlights athlete lifestyles including training routines, nutrition, discipline, education balance, and career development pathways.

Color Scheme

#1f6f4a – Primary brand color (headings, nav)

#1e88e5 – Secondary accent (buttons, highlights)

#f4fbff – Background color

Typography

Poppins will be used for all headings and titles.

Roboto will be used for body text and general content.

Open Sans will be used for highlighted text, buttons, and labels.

Wireframes

Weather widget (Kampala) — add API key in `styles/scripts/home.js` to enable.
