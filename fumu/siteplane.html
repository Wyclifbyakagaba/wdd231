01 — Site Name

Fumu Sports Foundation

The name Fumu Sports Foundation was chosen because it represents a sports development organization dedicated to athlete growth, community sports programs, youth empowerment, and charity initiatives. "Fumu" reflects community identity and strength in the local Ugandan context, while "Foundation" signals a structured, trustworthy organization committed to long-term impact across Uganda and East Africa.

02 — Site Purpose

What This Website Does

The website is the primary digital home of the Fumu Sports Foundation, providing:

  • Information on sports training programs for youth and adults across Uganda
  • Athlete rankings at local, East African, and international levels
  • Coaching staff profiles and credentials per sport
  • Upcoming competitions, leagues, and tournaments calendar
  • Sponsorship and partnership opportunities for businesses and organizations
  • Charity initiative details — free sports training for street children
  • Athlete lifestyle content: training, nutrition, education balance, and career paths
03 — Scenarios

Who Visits and Why

  • 🏃 "Which sports programs can young athletes join in Uganda, and what is the registration process?"
  • 🏆 "How are players ranked locally and internationally, and where can I see current standings?"
  • 💼 "How can my company sponsor sports development programs or charity initiatives in Uganda?"
  • 🤝 "Where can street children access free sports training and mentorship near me?"
