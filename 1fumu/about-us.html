About Us

Our mission, vision, and the people behind Fumu Sports Foundation.

Our Mission

Fumu Sports Foundation exists to develop young athletes across Uganda through structured training, mentorship, education, and competition. We believe sport is one of the most powerful tools for building confidence, discipline, and opportunity in the lives of young people.

Our Vision

To become East Africa's leading grassroots sports foundation, producing not only competitive athletes, but well-rounded young leaders who give back to their communities.

What We Do

  • Run football, basketball, boxing, and athletics training programs for youth and adults
  • Track and publish athlete rankings at local, regional, and international levels
  • Provide free sports access and mentorship to street children in Kampala
  • Partner with sponsors and businesses to fund equipment, travel, and coaching
